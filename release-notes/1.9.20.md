## New in 1.9.20
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Video calls:** the pictures of the others can stay in a small field above the text (four sizes, four corners) or go into a window of their own (always on top, full screen with a double click).
- **[Feature]** **Following:** the verse the other one clicks now comes into view, also in the chapter already open.
- **[Feature]** **Documents in the Explorer:** a document put into a folder shows with the marks of that folder, opens with a double click, moves and deletes like the rest.
- **[Feature]** **To-dos of all vaults:** a checkbox in the list and in the small window shows the to-dos of every vault, each under its vault's name; the choice is remembered.
- **[Feature]** **Microsoft Store version** (no own update search, updates come from the Store).

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
