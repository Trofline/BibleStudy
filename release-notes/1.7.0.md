## New in 1.7.0
**Kind of update:** Feature, Improvement, Bug fix
- **[Feature]** **Split view:** The new button with the split rectangle at the top puts up to four areas side by side, for example the Bible on the left and the document you are writing on the right. Drag a tab onto the middle of an area to replace it, or onto its edge to get a new area there; the lines between the areas can be dragged. The Bible text can be moved like any tab. The split view is off at first and stays as you left it.
- **[Feature]** **Materials:** A new tab shows the papers of a Bible study inside the program, so you no longer switch to Word with Alt+Tab. Choose a folder (with or without its sub-folders); its files show as a tree, a click opens one. Word (also the old .doc format, even folders of hundreds of pages), PDF (as page images or as text), PowerPoint, OpenDocument, RTF, text, Markdown, web pages and pictures open right there, other files in their own program. A table of contents in the file can be clicked to jump to its section, and a "Contents" button lists it with a search field. Tab switches between the open files, new files appear by themselves, folders can be opened and closed all at once and stay as you left them, and Bible references in a file open the passage: right in the text with a click, or from the buttons above it.
- **[Feature]** **Reading with the keyboard:** The arrow keys scroll the text or move from verse to verse, Shift+arrows choose several verses, left and right turn the chapter. Space shows the colors, the arrow keys pick one, Enter applies it, Esc closes them.
- **[Feature]** **Clock and timer:** The time stands at the bottom right; a click on it sets a timer. When it runs out, the window comes to the front.
- **[Improvement]** **Marks of all vaults at the top:** The switch for showing the marks of all vaults is now also in the top bar.
- **[Improvement]** **Auto-hidden taskbar:** A new setting lets an automatically hidden Windows taskbar come up over the maximized program when the mouse reaches the edge (off by default).
- **[Improvement]** **Commentaries easier to find:** Once turned on in the settings, they are there at once, without a restart. The verse overview shows the note on the clicked verse with a button for the whole commentary, and a right-click on a verse offers "Commentary on this verse".
- **[Bug fix]** Submenus no longer show a second, cut-off arrow.
- **[Bug fix]** Commentaries name the verses a note covers ("Verses 1–21"), mark the right note for the verse you click, and no longer show the note of another verse for a verse without one.
