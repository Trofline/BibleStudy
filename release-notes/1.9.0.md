## New in 1.9.0
**Kind of update:** Feature, Improvement
- **[Feature]** **Live Bible study on the phones:** The new Live tab shows a QR code (also large on the projector). Participants scan it and follow along in the browser of their phone, without an app, an account or the internet; they only have to be in the same Wi-Fi as your computer. The phones show what the projector shows and the handout; participants can quietly send you a question and vote in polls. You decide when the results appear on the phones or the projector.
- **[Feature]** **Archive of Bible studies:** Keep which passage was read on which evening, with notes and the questions that came up. A live Bible study can put itself into the archive when it ends, and above a chapter that was already read, the reader shows "In the Bible study on …".
- **[Feature]** **Discussion questions by method:** Documents → New → Discussion questions by method creates a document for the chosen verses with ready questions after six methods (Observe – Interpret – Apply, Discovery Bible Study, Swedish method, SOAP, Discover the story, Lectio Divina). In the handout the questions become a numbered box.
- **[Feature]** **Synopsis of the gospels:** More than 110 events and sayings as Matthew, Mark, Luke and John tell them, side by side in your translation. Words only one gospel has are colored.
- **[Feature]** **Church fathers:** What Augustine, John Chrysostom, Jerome, Origen and many others wrote on the verse you click, the oldest first (public domain collection, in English, loaded once on request, about 180 MB).
- **[Feature]** **Searching by meaning in the original text:** New search words: `Sense:` finds every word of the same meaning field (forgive → also grace, pardon …), `Refers:` finds where a person is named or meant (also "he", "him" and unwritten subjects), `LXX:` shows how the Septuagint renders Hebrew words and back, `Agree:` finds words that agree in case, number and gender, `Event:` the passages of an event of the timeline, and `Root:` all words of a Hebrew root.
- **[Feature]** **Notes from e-Sword:** Settings → Backup and sharing → Import notes from e-Sword takes over your notes and own commentaries from e-Sword or MySword: notes on verses become notes, topics become documents.
- **[Improvement]** The handbook explains all search possibilities, including wildcards, the Logos-style operators and the hit views.
