## New in 1.9.26
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Reading paths in the Explorer:** a reading path can belong to a folder. Open the folder and the path stands above the list with **▶ Read** and **Arrange**, and arrows in the margin lead from entry to entry in the order of the path. A new order of the steps or of the list draws them anew; a right click on an entry moves it earlier or later in the path. *New reading path from this folder* makes a path of all its entries.
- **[Feature]** **Add to the last path with one tap:** at the top of the verse menu, and on the phone in the color bar over chosen verses.
- **[Feature]** **Share reading paths:** export one path or all of them as a file, with or without the marks on their verses. Whoever reads the file in keeps the paths or only looks at them until the program closes, and either tints the verses with the marks or takes the marks over into the vault.
- **[Feature]** **Weigh the cross references in the graph beside the text:** printed and your own, only your own, or only the printed ones; a minimum number of votes; and how many votes your own count as. Your own cross references from all vaults count, and the setting belongs to the program, not to one vault.
- The handbook (German and English) is brought up to date.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
