## New in 1.6.10
**Kind of update:** New feature, Improvement
- **[New feature]** **Much more powerful search:** New operators, written in capitals: `BEFORE`, `AFTER`, `NEAR`, `WITHIN 3 WORDS`, `WITHIN 2 VERSES`, `WITHIN 1 SENTENCE` (also across verses), `WITHIN 1 CHAPTER`, `NOT WITHIN`, `NOT` and `ANDNOT`. `?` stands for exactly one letter, `*` also works inside a phrase ("angel * Lord"), and `~word` finds similar spellings. The search help has an example for each.
- **[New feature]** **Search only in certain passages:** Add `in:"Mt 5-7"`, `in:John` or `in:"Rom 1-8; Gal"` to any search.
- **[New feature]** **Grammar and words together:** `Gram:"verb aorist passive" grace` finds the verses whose Greek or Hebrew text has such a form and that also contain the word. The matching original-language words are shown below each hit.
- **[New feature]** **Five views of the results:** Verses, Aligned (every found word under each other with its context), Grid (books against translations), Compare (the verses side by side in several translations) and Analysis (spread across the Bible, word forms found, chapters with the most hits). Up to five more translations can be searched at the same time.
- **[Improvement]** **Clause search:** The word field now also takes `Jesus / Peter` (one of them), `-Peter` (not this word in the role) and the placeholders `*` and `?`.
- **[Improvement]** **More room for the hits:** The actions for the hits (document, table, map, word statistics) are now together under "Hits ▾".
