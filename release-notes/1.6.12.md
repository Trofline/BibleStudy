## New in 1.6.12
**Kind of update:** Bug fix, Improvement
- **[Bug fix]** Exporting a vault failed with "file in use" because of the shared reading progress; it works again.
- **[Improvement]** **PDF import of printed Bibles is much more reliable:** Chapters whose first verse was swallowed by the verse before (for example Genesis 1:1, James 1:1, Exodus 7:1) are now complete, and chapters that were lost whole (Joshua 20, Revelation 1) are back. Pages that a PDF contains twice are read once (2 Corinthians to 1 Thessalonians were doubled). Titles in capital letters become headings, letters that the print set apart from their word ("gin g en", "An stoßes", "Unter- weisung") are put back, verse numbers set a little larger than the text are found (Jeremiah 20:7-8), and stray capitals like "MutterHERRNHERRN" are gone.
