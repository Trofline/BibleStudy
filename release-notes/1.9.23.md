## New in 1.9.23
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Word differences:** with parallel columns in one language, a new switch "Unterschiede" tints the words that differ from the neighbouring column.
- **[Feature]** **Marks by colour:** a new button in the marks list groups them by colour instead of by folder, so every verse of one colour is together.
- **[Feature]** **Read a Markdown folder:** an Obsidian vault or an earlier export is read into the vault (Settings → My data, or the Explorer menu). Files become documents, folders stay folders, notes of the export become notes again. Nothing is replaced: a document with the same title is merged line by line. A backup is made first.
- **[Feature]** **Reading reminder:** once a day from a time you choose, if nothing was read yet. On a computer a small window appears at the bottom right, also when the program is in the tray; on a phone when the app is opened.
- **[Feature]** **Shortcut from anywhere (Windows):** Ctrl+Alt+B (or another of four) brings BibleStudy to the front, also out of the tray.
- **[Fix]** The verse of the day has its own tint that can be seen.
- **[Fix]** iPhone: the bar of an extra column fits the screen (the x was off the screen) and takes less height.
- **[Fix]** iPhone: the book list on the start page has two columns right after the start.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
