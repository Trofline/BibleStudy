## New in 1.6.16
**Kind of update:** Improvement, Mini feature, Bug fix
- **[Improvement]** **Deleting can be undone:** Notes and folders now go into the trash like marks and documents, and emptying the reading list can be taken back. Right after deleting, a bar at the bottom offers "Undo".
- **[Improvement]** **Messages you should not miss appear as a bar:** A chapter that cannot be loaded (no connection) shows a bar with "Try again", and the book and chapter at the top stay on the text you see. A failed automatic backup and verses sent to the reading list show a bar too, instead of only a line in the status bar.
- **[Improvement]** **Keyboard:** Switching between reading and your chosen tab is now Ctrl+Tab. The Tab key moves from button to button again, so the program can be used without a mouse. Question windows answer to Enter and close with Esc.
- **[Improvement]** **Easy mode shows what it promises:** search, verse overview, notes and bookmarks.
- **[Improvement]** **First start:** The program opens a Bible in its own language (English, Spanish, French or Russian) instead of always the German one.
- **[Mini feature]** **Search in the translation picker:** Type to find a translation, helpful for languages with many of them.
- **[Mini feature]** **Trash in Ctrl+K:** Type "Trash" to open it, also for deleted documents.
- **[Bug fix]** Text variants now show for 1–2 Corinthians, 1–2 Thessalonians, 1–2 Timothy, 1–2 Peter and 1–3 John.
- **[Bug fix]** The search works on texts that contain emoji or other rare characters.
- **[Bug fix]** Settings are kept when another program holds the settings file at start.
- **[Bug fix]** Undoing a mark keeps the places corrected by hand in other translations.
- **[Bug fix]** Deleting the only vault removes all of its content.
- **[Bug fix]** Saving a note works after clicking beside the text.
- **[Bug fix]** "mal 3,5" or "hab 3,5" in a note no longer become Bible links.
- **[Bug fix]** Ctrl+→ marks the chapter as read, like the arrow button.
- **[Bug fix]** Studying together survives a short Wi-Fi drop.
- **[Bug fix]** Dates follow the language of the program.
- **[Bug fix]** The status bar stays visible under the mouse.
- **[Bug fix]** Ctrl+K "Languages" opens the language page.
- **[Bug fix]** Imported Bibles get the language of the program by default.
- **[Bug fix]** The explorer no longer mixes up notes of two books.
- **[Bug fix]** License labels in the translation picker and the status bar are translated.
