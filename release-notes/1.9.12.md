## New in 1.9.12
**Kind of update:** Feature, Bug fix
- **[Feature]** **Tidier settings:** they start with a search and eight tiles (Appearance, Reading, Bibles and data, My data, Sync, Windows and start, AI, Help and updates). The search lists single settings and jumps right to them. Every setting is still there.
- **[Feature]** **Download manager shows sizes:** each entry shows about how much space it takes, and below you see what is ticked, what is still to download and how much is free. On phones "Select all" leaves out the very large Hebrew syntax data.
- **[Feature]** **Closing the PC waits for your phone:** if the phone has not got your latest changes yet, the program stays open and asks you to open BibleStudy on the phone for a moment, then closes by itself. "Close now" ends it at once. Can be switched off under Sync.
- **[Feature]** **Sync in three steps:** the sync settings show how to pair a phone, step by step.
- **[Feature]** **This week's reading** on the home page: chapters of the last seven days and your streak.
- **[Feature]** **Several verses as one image:** mark up to six verses and export them as one picture; on phones it opens the share sheet.
- **[Feature]** **After a crash** the next start offers to report the error with one tap.
- **[Feature]** iPhone: an optional warning before the app expires (off by default; the shortcut normally re-signs it in time).
- **[Bug fix]** Studying together did not connect over ZeroTier or Tailscale: the invitation only offered the address of the main network.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
