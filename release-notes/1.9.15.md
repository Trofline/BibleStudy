## New in 1.9.15
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Study in groups:** friends, a leader, questions, a shared pad, reactions and live highlights.
- **[Feature]** **Voice and video on the PC:** push-to-talk, calls and camera while studying together.
- **[Feature]** **Pictures in documents:** add pictures from files; they are kept inside the vault, shown in the reading view, printed, and saved with a Markdown export.
- **[Feature]** **See everything at once:** "Show all" in the explorer, documents and marks of all vaults, and shared vaults whose content shows in every vault.
- **[Feature]** **Copy only the place:** Ctrl+C on a selected verse copies the reference (e.g. "Genesis 15,2"); Ctrl+Shift+C copies the quote with the place, as before.
- **[Feature]** The Tab key switches between the tabs (Reading, documents, ...).
- **[Feature]** The update notice has a button "Update in SideStore" that installs the new version directly.
- **[Fix]** Dragging a window out (e.g. search) no longer makes it disappear.
- **[Fix]** Clicking a verse number in a selection colours all selected verses; recolouring a marked verse recolours its whole group.
- **[Fix]** Black lines inside marks in Word study mode.
- **[Fix]** The "..." menu of documents was empty.
- **[Fix]** Patch notes: the close button is at the top.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
