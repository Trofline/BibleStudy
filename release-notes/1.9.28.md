## New in 1.9.28
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[New]** Reading list: **Paste list …** takes a list of passages copied from a web page or an e-mail (one per line, with bullets or the verse text after them) and adds them verse by verse.
- **[New]** Marks of whole verses that follow each other can be merged into one mark, and a mark over several verses can be split into single verses again (Explorer and verse menu).
- **[New]** Marking text right behind or over a mark of the same colour (the forgotten "!") extends that mark instead of adding a second one.
- **[New]** A reading path made from a folder counts several marks in one verse as one step.
- **[New]** A click on the number of a step in the margin opens that reading path; "Order" is now called "Edit".
- **[Fix]** Choosing a book no longer jumps to chapter 1 before you pick a chapter (the last place stays the last place).
- **[Fix]** A deleted "Standard" vault no longer comes back with the old marks.
- **[Change]** Phone: the button top left is now a "recently read" icon, so it cannot be mistaken for "previous chapter".

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
