## New in 1.6.17
**Kind of update:** Improvement, Security, Bug fix
- **[Improvement]** **Reading bar with labels:** Graph, map, timeline and family tree now share one "Views" menu, so the bar has room for its labels again. In a narrower window the labels give way one by one, the least needed first, instead of all at once.
- **[Improvement]** **Faster with many notes:** Clicking a verse no longer reloads all notes, Ctrl+K waits until you stop typing and searches your own data in the background, and clicking in the explorer no longer rebuilds the whole list. Backups before deleting or merging a vault no longer freeze the window.
- **[Improvement]** **Clearer names:** The mode switch is named in the language of the program (German "Einfach" and "Komplett"), the rail shows "Explorer" for the explorer of marks and notes, and language names appear in their own language ("Deutsch", "Español").
- **[Improvement]** **Settings and help:** The "Appearance" settings are grouped into colors, font and text, original text, and view and motion. Your own colors can be typed as a color code, and the sliders are labeled. The help page opens section by section and has a search field.
- **[Improvement]** **Calmer window:** Tabs show their dot and close button only when open or under the mouse, the side panel folds away by itself in a narrow window and comes back when there is room, and the long hints under the panels moved into tooltips.
- **[Security]** **Relay password:** On Windows it is now stored encrypted for your account instead of as plain text in the settings and backups. After restoring a backup on another computer, type it in again.
- **[Security]** **Plugins:** An allowed plugin is asked about again when any of its program files changes, not only its plugin.json. Plugins you already allowed have to be allowed once more.
- **[Security]** The reading voice (Piper) is checked against a fixed checksum before it is unpacked and started.
- **[Bug fix]** Enter in a question window no longer picks the first answer when there are several (it could delete a vault).
- **[Bug fix]** Document folders and categories use the same symbols as the rest of the program.
