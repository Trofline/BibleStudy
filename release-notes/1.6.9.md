## New in 1.6.9
**Kind of update:** New feature, Improvement, Bug fix
- **[New feature]** **Seamless updates:** When a quick update is ready you now choose between "Install now", "Install when closing" and "Later". "Install now" restarts the program in about two seconds and puts you back exactly where you were: same window, tabs, chapter, verse and scroll position, and instead of the patch notes only a short note appears. "Install when closing" downloads in the background while you keep working and installs when you quit the program. A version that fails to start is taken back automatically. (This applies to updates from this version on.)
- **[Improvement]** **Small screens and laptops:** The program window can now be much smaller (down to 850 wide) and never starts bigger than the screen. What does not fit in the top bar or the reading bar moves behind a "…" button instead of hiding behind other buttons; the reading bar shows icons only when it gets tight. Other windows stay on the screen and scroll when they are too tall.
- **[Improvement]** **Installer:** The setup window now scales with the screen scaling (125 %, 150 % …) and has more room for its text.
- **[Bug fix]** The buttons in the AI tab and in the notes tab no longer overlap in a narrow side panel.
- **[Bug fix]** The first text in the installer is no longer cut off.
