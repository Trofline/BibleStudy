## New in 1.9.21
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Voice notes on a verse:** record with the microphone of the computer, Android or iPhone from the verse menu (listen, name, keep or discard; up to 5 minutes). A ♪ next to the verse number; play and delete from the verse menu and the verse overview. They are kept in the vault, so they synchronise between devices.
- **[Feature]** **Side by side:** a column with a passage of its own works like the main text. Click or tap chooses a verse, right click or long press opens its menu (mark the verse or the selected words, note, bookmark, voice note, copy, read aloud from here, open as main text and more); its marks, stars and notes show in the column. A typed verse number goes to the column touched last.
- **[Feature]** **Explorer:** marks and notes can be copied or moved into another vault (with color, note, tags and their folder). Long folder names end in … in the tree and the path. On a phone the bar takes two rows.
- **[Feature]** **Tab key:** switches between reading and the last tab. A dot on a tab sets which one; with two dots Tab switches between those two. Ctrl+Tab goes through all tabs.
- **[Feature]** **Trash:** a button above the documents and in the bar after deleting opens the trash.
- **[Feature]** **Much better import:** PDF Bibles now bring their footnotes, whole psalm titles, verses the print numbers itself (like [8,23]) and words with a dash for a left-out part ("Schlacht- und Speisopfer"); words the PDF split ("kön nen") are whole again and parallel passages stay out of the text. Pages of csv-bibel.de keep every paragraph of a verse (poems, songs, sayings). Imported Bibles are offered once to be read again; your marks stay with their words.
- Synchronising no longer makes copies of a document changed on both devices.
- The start page no longer shows the book and chapter of the last reading.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
