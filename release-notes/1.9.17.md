## New in 1.9.17
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Go to a page in materials:** a page box beside the open file (Ctrl+G) jumps to a page of a PDF, Word or OpenDocument file and shows the current page while scrolling.
- **[Feature]** **More room for materials:** a button folds the bars above the file away, so the file gets the whole height.
- **[Feature]** **All ways of writing Bible references become links:** short forms (1Kö, 2Kö, 1Mo, 1Chron, Ezek …), ranges into the next chapter (Jes 44,27–45,3), verse parts (4b), lists (Dan 6,9.13.16), f./ff., and places without a book after ";" or "(" that take the book just named.
- **[Feature]** While a second click on X waits for the phone, the X counts down the seconds.
- **[Fix]** Parallel passages at the start of a verse in imported Bibles (e.g. Schlachter Jes 39,1) are shown as a footnote.
- **[Fix]** Popped-out windows no longer vanish on PCs with screens of different scaling.
- **[Fix]** The Satzdiagramm and Perikope buttons above the text open the Studium area again.
- **[Fix]** The Bible text is smooth again (letters were pixelated).

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
