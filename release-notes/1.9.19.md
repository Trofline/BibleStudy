## New in 1.9.19
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Exact search:** umlauts and ß count as letters of their own (schon is not schön, das is not daß). An option ignores them when wanted.
- **[Feature]** **Found word forms:** the forms found, with their counts, stand above the hits; a click shows only that form. The list holds every hit instead of the first 400.
- **[Feature]** **Base forms (L=) that are exact:** only real German endings count (L=gehen no longer finds geheim, L=Haus finds Häuser but not Haushalter, L=Vater finds Väter). English and Spanish Bibles get base forms too, other languages by their infinitive ending.
- **[Feature]** **Person: and Ort: understand German names** (Isaak, Mose, Petrus, Ägypten), take the person meant most often, say which one was taken, and add the verses whose text names them.
- **[Feature]** **Strong numbers in Bibles without them** are found through the Greek and Hebrew text instead of reporting no hits.
- **[Feature]** in:"Röm 1-8; Gal" mixes passages and whole books.
- **[Feature]** **Marks in the materials synchronise** between PC and phone, deletions included.
- **[Fix]** O=grace in an English Bible found him/them/his; now only the real renderings.
- **[Fix]** The words of Jesus say when chapters could not be loaded.
- **[Fix]** Texts that store ü as u + dots are found and marked.
- **[Fix]** A similar spelling (~) no longer counts an umlaut as a mistake.
- **[Fix]** The same words marked twice in a file are one mark.
- **[Fix]** Digits of other scripts no longer break Bible references, imports or searches.
- **[Fix]** A note of another vault in the Explorer can no longer delete or move your own.
- **[Fix]** A new document without a title is kept under its first line.
- **[Fix]** Big photos in documents are stored as JPEG.
- **[Fix]** Deleting the only vault also empties the marks in the materials, the pictures and the reading progress.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
