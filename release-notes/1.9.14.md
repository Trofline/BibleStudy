## New in 1.9.14
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **See each other live while studying together:** when you both read the same chapter, the verses the other person clicks get a purple frame with their name, and their mouse pointer moves over your text. You also see when they are typing. Can be switched off with "Show my mouse pointer".
- **[Feature]** **Share more:** highlights of the chosen verses, the chapter, the book or all of them; notes of the chapter, the book or all of them; and a copy of a whole vault, which the other person can view as a guest or save as a new vault. Taking over adds only what is new and never overwrites anything.
- **[Feature]** **Show here:** the other person gets a notice with a button to your place. Chat lines show a small notice while the page is closed.
- **[Feature]** Connecting with codes across different networks is now marked as beta; the tested and reliable way is the same network, e.g. with ZeroTier.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
