## New in 1.9.7
**Kind of update:** Feature, Bug fix
- **[Feature]** **Everything in one release:** Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone and the plugins are now all here. Android updates itself from these releases from now on.
- **[Feature]** **Sync you can see:** a notice "Connected to …" appears when your devices reach each other, and "Device paired" right after scanning the QR code.
- **[Feature]** **Sync reminder:** after 3 days without a sync the app reminds you to open it on the other device. Nothing gets lost meanwhile: marks made apart on both devices all arrive on both.
- **[Feature]** Handbook 19.4: Shortcuts automations for the iPhone (sync when you get home, renew the app every night).
- **[Bug fix]** The SideStore source offers iPhone updates again.

**iPhone:** add the SideStore source `https://trofline.github.io/BibleStudy/sidestore.json` (or install the `.ipa` below with SideStore).
