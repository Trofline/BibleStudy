## New in 1.6.15
**Kind of update:** New feature, Bug fix
- **[New feature]** **Walk through the reading list:** "Start" in the reading list opens a small strip at the bottom left with previous, next and the whole list, so you no longer have to change tabs. It covers no text. Verses you visited leave the list only when you finish, so you can still go back.
- **[Bug fix]** Typing a verse number with two digits no longer jumps to the wrong verse.
- **[Bug fix]** An update no longer interrupts the setup on the first start.
- **[Bug fix]** A link to several verses, like Exodus 20:8-11, now selects all of them, not only the first.
