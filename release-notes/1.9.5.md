## New in 1.9.5
**Kind of update:** Feature, Bug fix
- **[Feature]** **Sync your devices:** Settings → Sync devices. Pair your PC with your phone or another PC once (QR code or a 26-character code). In the same Wi-Fi a change reaches the other device within seconds, without an account and without a server in between; the PC sends its last changes when it closes. The side that changed something wins, deletions go into the trash on the other device too, and a new or empty device never deletes anything.
- **[Feature]** **BibleStudy on the phone (beta):** Android and iPhone versions with a layout made for small screens. See the releases marked android-beta and ios-beta.
- **[Feature]** Mac: shortcuts use ⌘.
- **[Bug fix]** Mac: the arc graph showed only a quarter.
