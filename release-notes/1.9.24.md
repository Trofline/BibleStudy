## New in 1.9.24
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Own commentaries:** BibleStudy no longer brings any commentaries and no longer loads the Church Fathers collection; what a commentary teaches is for you to judge. Instead you read in your own: e-Sword (.cmtx), MySword (.cmt.mybible) or a Markdown or text file in which a line with a Bible reference (## John 3:16) starts the entry. They show in the tab, in the verse overview and in the search, and stay on your computer.
- **[Feature]** **Shortcut from anywhere (Windows):** it is on from the start and sits in the list of shortcuts (Ctrl+Alt+B, changeable like the others). The program waits until the keys are let go, and a window brought forward is not minimized again at once.
- **[Fix]** The button "Als Markdown kopieren" in the chapter report and "Standard" in the column colours are no longer cut off.
- The handbook (German and English) is brought up to date.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
