## New in 1.6.14
**Kind of update:** New feature, Improvement, Bug fix
- **[New feature]** **Take your vaults along:** "Export vaults" in the settings writes the vaults you tick into one ZIP, with marks, notes, documents, lists and reading progress, but without Bible texts. Another computer opens it with "Import" or "Import as guest".
- **[New feature]** **Bring guest work home:** If you changed something in a guest vault, the program offers to save it as a ZIP before closing. Imported at home, it can be merged into the vault of the same name. Nothing is deleted or overwritten, where both differ both are kept, and a backup is made first.
- **[New feature]** **A look before importing:** Importing a vault file first shows what it holds and which translations are missing on this computer.
- **[New feature]** **See what changes when a Bible is replaced:** Importing a Bible you already have shows the changed, new and removed verses before the old text is replaced.
- **[Improvement]** **Marks across translations:** A part of a verse marked in a translation that is not on this computer is found by its words. A small ° on a verse says that a mark cannot be shown there. A place that is only estimated is underlined with dashes, and a click lets you correct it.
- **[Improvement]** Reading imported Bibles again finds files that were moved.
- **[Bug fix]** The import check no longer reports verses a translation leaves out on purpose, or ordinary word pairs as split words.
