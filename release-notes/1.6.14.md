## New in 1.6.14
**Kind of update:** New feature, Improvement, Bug fix
- **[New feature]** **Take your vaults along:** "Export vaults" in the settings writes the vaults you tick into one ZIP, with marks, notes, documents, lists and reading progress. Imported Bibles go along only if you tick them. Another computer opens the ZIP with "Import" or "Import as guest"; a Bible that came with a guest import is gone again when the program closes.
- **[New feature]** **Bring your work home:** If you changed something in a guest vault, the program offers to save it as a ZIP before closing. Any vault file whose name already exists here can be merged into that vault. Nothing is deleted or overwritten, and a backup is made first. Afterwards you choose mine, theirs or both where the two differ, decide whether things deleted as a guest should go here too, and can undo the whole merge.
- **[New feature]** **A look before importing:** Importing a vault file first shows what it holds and which translations are missing on this computer.
- **[New feature]** **See what changes when a Bible is replaced:** Importing a Bible you already have shows the changed, new and removed verses first. A verse you untick keeps its old text.
- **[New feature]** **Correct with AI:** After an import with reading errors, a button hands the suspicious verses to Claude. The suggestions appear verse by verse before anything changes, and a verse that was reworded too much is refused.
- **[Improvement]** **Marks across translations:** A part of a verse marked in a translation that is not on this computer is found by its words, also over several verses. A small ° on a verse says that a mark cannot be shown there. A place that is only estimated is underlined with dashes, and a click lets you correct it.
- **[Improvement]** Reading imported Bibles again finds files that were moved.
- **[Bug fix]** The import check no longer reports verses a translation leaves out on purpose, or ordinary word pairs as split words.
