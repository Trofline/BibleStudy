## New in 1.9.25
**Kind of update:** Feature
- **[Feature]** **Reading paths:** link verses or marks in the order they are best read, so one builds on the next. Arrows in the left margin show the way from step to step, numbered circles show the position, and chips under a verse lead to the step before and after. Start one from the verse menu (Lesepfad) or from several entries chosen in the Explorer; rename, recolour and reorder in the manager. Paths are synced with your other devices.
- **[Feature]** **Several whole verses on the phone:** hold your finger on a verse number and move it down to choose the verses below; lifting it shows the colours for all of them.
- **[Feature]** **Choose several entries in the Explorer:** tap Auswählen (or long press an entry on the phone), tick the others, then move, colour, tag, delete them all at once or make a reading path from them.
- **[Fix]** Removing the mark of a whole verse also removes its colour (on the iPhone the colour stayed).
- **[Fix]** Documents of other vaults ("Dokumente aller Vaults zeigen") open on a tap on the phone.
- The README explains the iPhone install more simply, and why BibleStudy is not in the App Store or Play Store.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
