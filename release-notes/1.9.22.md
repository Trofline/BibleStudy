## New in 1.9.22
**Kind of update:** Bug fix
- Importing a PDF Bible no longer fails with "Value must be set" when a footnote letter has no note at the page foot (Schlachter 2000, Jeremiah 4:3).

If 1.9.21 could not read your PDF Bible again: Settings → Bibles and data → Choose file … and pick the PDF once more.

**iPhone:** update in SideStore (Sources → pull down → My Apps → Update). **Android:** the app updates itself.
