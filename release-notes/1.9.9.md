## New in 1.9.9
**Kind of update:** Feature, Bug fix
- **[Feature]** **Everything syncs:** besides marks and notes now also tasks, bookmarks, the reading list, prayer requests, headings, your own cross-references, studies, memory verses, vocabulary, the reading plan, categories, colors and saved searches.
- **[Feature]** **Changes show at once:** what another device synced appears on the home page, in the reading view and in the lists without restarting.
- **[Feature]** **Cloud folder:** sync through OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or any shared folder; the devices need not be on at the same time.
- **[Feature]** The PC and the phones sync once more when the app is closed or sent to the background.
- **[Feature]** **iPhone:** a ready-made shortcut switches the SideStore VPN on and off and renews the app every 6 days. Updates open SideStore for you.
- **[Feature]** On phones, features that work only on a PC are hidden.
- **[Bug fix]** The iPhone did not save and sync when going to the background.
- **[Bug fix]** Buttons overlapped in the download manager on phones.
- **[Bug fix]** On phones, "Check for updates" showed no answer; on the iPhone a new version now shows a notice with "Open SideStore".

**iPhone:** see the new step-by-step guide in the README. **Android:** the app updates itself.
