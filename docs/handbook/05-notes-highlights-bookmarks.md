# 5. Notes, Highlights and Bookmarks

Everything you write or mark yourself belongs to the **active vault** (your folder, see [Chapter 15](15-backup-privacy.md)). This chapter explains the three simplest tools.

## 5.1 The differences at a glance

| Tool | Purpose | Has a note? | Has a color? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Note** | a thought about a verse | yes | no |
| **Highlight** | emphasize a word or verse in color | optional | yes, with folders and tags |
| **Bookmark** | mark a passage so you can jump back to it | no | no (just a star ★) |

## 5.2 Notes

1. Click a verse.
2. Right sidebar → **Notes** (or right-click → *Write note*).
3. Write the note. It is saved automatically.

Verses with a note show a ✎ next to the verse number. Below, in the tab, you'll find all your notes; clicking one jumps to the passage.

## 5.3 Highlights

There are two kinds:

- **Word or piece of text:** Select text with the mouse, then **Highlight** (or right-click → *Highlight (choose color) …*).
- **Whole verse:** Click the verse, then **Highlight**.

You choose a color; you set the default color in the Settings. A highlight can carry its own **note**.

### Folders, tags, categories

Under *Notes → Highlights* (the collection) you organize highlights into **folders** and give them **tags**. If you like, the program assigns the **book name as a tag** when saving (setting). Clicking an entry jumps to the passage.

## 5.4 Bookmarks

Bookmarks are the simplest list: verses you want to jump back to later, without a note.

- **Set:** Click a verse, then **Bookmark** at the top or **Ctrl+D**. Pressing it once more removes it. Also in the verse menu: *Bookmark / remove bookmark*.
- **Star:** A bookmarked verse shows a ★ next to the verse number.
- **List:** Right sidebar → **Bookmarks**. Clicking an entry jumps to the passage; the ✕ removes the bookmark.

In older versions, bookmarks were called "Merkliste". They now have their own group in the sidebar.

> **Which one when?** If you just want to find again what you're currently reading: bookmark. If you want to capture something: note. If you want to emphasize a word or sort things: highlight. If the verse should become a task: to-do (see [Chapter 7](07-planning.md)).

## 5.5 Where do I find everything I've written about a verse?

In the **Verse overview** (Ctrl+G). It shows your notes, documents and highlights for that verse. With **Ctrl+K** you can also find all your own material (documents, notes, highlights with their notes and tags, prayers, to-dos, your own cross-references) through a single field.

## 5.6 Backing up and taking things with you

Everything lives in the vault and is included in every backup. For sharing:

- *Settings → Export …* writes the active vault, including settings, as a ZIP; *Import …* creates a **new** vault from it.
- *Export as Obsidian vault …* writes documents, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading list and to-dos as a Markdown folder that Obsidian opens directly.
