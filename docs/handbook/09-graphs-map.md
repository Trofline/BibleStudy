# 9. Graphs, Map, Timeline and Family Tree

The buttons **Graphs**, **Map**, **Timeline** and **Family tree** above the text show the Bible as a picture. They are hidden in Easy mode (Graphs) or only visible in Advanced mode.

## 9.1 Cross-references in the text: "View"

The **View** button shows cross-references in the text and displays a **graph next to the text**: threads connect the verses you are currently looking at with their cross-references. The graph stays fixed next to the scrolling text.

## 9.2 The "Graphs" button

It has several views:

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Network** | a chapter and its references; expandable by double-clicking a node |
| **Arc** | the whole Bible, with references as arcs |
| **OT ↔ NT** | references between the Testaments |
| **Circle** | all books in a circle, connected by ribbons |
| **Writers** | the Bible writers by traditional attribution |
| **Word network** | words that often appear in the same verses |
| **People network** | who appears in the same chapters |
| **Topic network** | related words of a topic |

### Using the networks

- **Dragging a node** moves it.
- **The mouse wheel** zooms.
- **Clicking a node:** its neighbors light up.
- **Double-clicking:** loads more neighbors.

### Connecting several terms

In the word, people and topic networks, you can connect several terms with **+**, for example "day + Lord" or "Peter + John". The network then shows only what fits all of them together: verses or chapters, respectively, in which all of them appear.

### What the networks are based on

- The **Word network** is based on Strong's numbers (KJV).
- The **People network** is based on Theographic: people who appear in the same chapters.
- The **Topic network** uses German synonyms and counts how often they share a verse in the text you've read.

## 9.3 Reference network (OT ↔ NT)

**Ctrl+K → "Reference network"** shows the cross-references between OT and NT books as a picture. Clicking a book shows its verses.

## 9.4 Map

The **Map** button shows the places in the chapter or book. Important:

- **Show journey:** For a person, the map shows the places mentioned in connection with them, in the order of the Bible.
- **Places of the search:** Under the search results, "Places on the map" shows where the places mentioned are located (larger dot = more verses).

The place data comes from the free sources listed under *Sources and licenses* (including Theographic).

## 9.5 Timeline

The **Timeline** button shows the timeline of the Bible with map. If you select an event, the map shows its places.

## 9.6 Family tree

The **Family tree** button shows a person's family tree. Choose a person, and the relationships (parents, children, spouses) are drawn as a tree.

## 9.7 Strategies

- **Understanding a term:** Open the Word network, look at the neighbors, and double-click whatever surprises you.
- **Understanding a passage:** The chapter's network; strongly connected verses are often key passages.
- **Understanding a person:** First the family tree, then the People network, then the map with the journey.
- **The big picture:** Arc or Circle. The many ribbons between OT and NT show how Scripture interprets itself.
