# 2. Reading and navigating

## 2.1 Opening a passage

There are three ways:

1. **Home:** the house icon (Alt+Home). All books, with a progress bar for each book. Click a book to see its chapters.
2. **Selection bar:** you choose language, translation, book and chapter with the buttons. The Book button shows *all books at a glance*, the Chapter button *all chapters at a glance*.
3. **Go to … (Ctrl+K):** type "Rom 8:28" and press Enter.

With ◀ ▶ (or Ctrl+← / Ctrl+→) you page through chapter by chapter, even across book boundaries. **Alt+←** jumps back to the previous passage, just like the Back button in a browser.

## 2.2 Read marking and progress

- The **Read** button (Ctrl+R) marks the chapter as read.
- On the Home page, a bar for each book shows your progress. A section (for example "Gospels") can be marked as read or reset as a whole.
- In the settings you can choose that a chapter counts as read automatically when you page on with ▶ or have scrolled to the end.

Your reading progress belongs to the active **vault** (see [Chapter 15](15-backup-privacy.md)).

## 2.3 Translations

The available translations depend on the free sources; you can see which ones are currently on offer in the translation button, grouped by language. Each translation has its own license, which is shown in the footer. If it is not public domain, the address of the license text is included when you copy a verse.

**Own names:** right-click a translation in the translation button → *Rename …*. You assign a **name** and a **short name** (the short name appears at the top of the bar), for example "Luther Bible (Study)" and "Luther-S". This helps most when you have several similar or imported translations. Empty fields keep the original name; *Restore the original name* undoes the change. The names apply throughout the whole interface, including the parallel view.

**Import your own translation:** *Settings → Import own translation → Choose file …*. Imported texts stay on your computer and are intended for your private use only. Under "Imported translations" you can remove them again.

## 2.4 Clicking verses

A click on a verse **selects it**. Everything that relates to a verse relates to the selected one: writing a note, setting a bookmark, cross-references, the verse overview.

**Right-clicking a verse** opens the verse menu:

| Entry | Effect |
|---|---|
| Verse overview | Everything about the verse at a glance |
| Copy marked text | (only if you have marked text) copies exactly what is marked |
| Copy verse with reference | "Text" – Passage (translation); for texts that are not public domain, with the license address |
| Export as picture | turns the verse into a picture |
| For memorizing | queues the verse up for training |
| Highlight (choose color) … | colors the verse |
| Bookmark / remove bookmark | bookmark |
| Write note | jumps to the note field |
| New document from this verse | starts a document with the verse |
| Insert into open document | inserts the verse as a quote |
| To reading list | appends the verse to the reading list |
| Save as to-do | creates a to-do "Study passage" with the verse |
| Cross-references | opens the cross-references |
| Add own cross reference … | links the verse to another passage |
| Original text word by word | shows the original text |
| Explain word | explains the marked word (only if one is marked) |
| Variant readings of the Greek editions | (New Testament only) |
| Read aloud | reads aloud |

> **Note:** Previously, when you right-clicked without a selection, a small "Copy Ctrl+C" button was laid over this menu. This is fixed: the verse menu stays on top, and copying marked text is now in the menu itself.

## 2.5 Parallel: several translations side by side

The **Parallel** button adds another translation as a column, up to three additional ones. Each column has its **own color** (header, tint, bar and dot in the bar), so you can tell the translations apart.

### The parallel bar

A bar appears above the text:

- **Scroll in sync:** On: all columns run side by side verse by verse and scroll together. Off: each column scrolls on its own.
- **Selection icon (three lines):** chooses which columns scroll together (for example only the second and fourth); the others scroll on their own.
- **Color button (palette):** changes the color of each column (see below).
- **Presets ▾:** saves the arrangement of the columns and loads it again.
- Per column: a **dot** (drag to move), **‹ ›** (column to the left/right), language, translation, a **passage button** and **✕** (remove column).

### A separate passage per column

A column's passage button initially says "Same as left". If you choose a different book and chapter there, the column reads a **separate passage**, for example Genesis next to Daniel. Such a column then runs on its own. It is meant for reading and word study; its verse numbers do not match those of the main text.

### Changing column colors

The color button in the parallel bar opens a list with one row per column:

- **Ten round colors** to click,
- a field for your **own hex value** (`#RRGGBB`, for example `#3B82F6`),
- **Default**, to return to the original color.

The color is tied to the column's **position** (main column, second, third, fourth) and is saved. By default the main column uses the accent color; the others are turquoise, violet and pink.

## 2.6 Word study in the text

The **Word study** switch underlines words that have a Strong's number (in translations that come with them, for example KJV). Clicking an underlined word opens the word study on the right. More on this in [Chapter 4](04-original-text-word-study.md).

## 2.7 Footnotes

Words with a footnote are clickable in the text; the footnote appears in a small window next to the word.

## 2.8 Reading aids

Under *Settings → Appearance* you will find:

- **Font size** (also A−/A+ at the top, Ctrl+Plus/Minus, Ctrl+0 resets),
- **Line spacing**, **Justified text**,
- **Easier-to-read font** (plain, with more letter spacing, for example for dyslexia),
- **High contrast**,
- **Verse numbers**: small and raised, normal in the text, or hidden,
- Highlight the **words of Jesus in color**, with a color of your own,
- for Hebrew: show or hide **vowel marks** and **accent marks**,
- **Light / Dark / System** (Ctrl+T switches),
- **Focus mode** (F11): only the text, everything else disappears.

## 2.9 Read aloud

The **Read aloud** button reads aloud the verse you clicked or the whole chapter. While it reads, a bar appears at the bottom with **−**, **Speed** and **+**: slower or faster (a click on “Speed” goes back to normal; the Windows voice goes on at the same place). **×** stops. In the settings (“Read aloud: voice”) you choose the voice: the **Windows voice** (sounds robotic) or a **natural voice** made with Piper. The natural voice runs offline on your computer (Windows, 64-bit only) and is downloaded the first time (about 85 MB). It exists for German (Thorsten), English (LJ Speech), Spanish (DaveFX), French (SIWIS) and Russian (Denis), all freely licensed. For other languages the Windows voice is used.

## 2.9a Audio Bible

The **Listen** button plays a **spoken recording** of the chapter you are reading. Unlike Read aloud this is not a computer voice but a real speaker: the **World English Bible**, read by Winfred W. Henson. Text and recording are in the public domain and are hosted by eBible.org.

- A player appears at the bottom: **previous chapter**, **pause / resume**, **next chapter**, a slider to jump around, and the time. The **×** closes the audio Bible.
- At the end of a chapter it continues with the next one, across book borders as well. The text in the reader turns the page with it as long as it shows the chapter that is playing. If “Mark chapter as read when turning forward (▶)” is on in the settings, a chapter you listened to the end is marked as read.
- The recording is **English**, even if you read a German translation; the columns next to it stay in your translation. All 66 books are recorded, but there are no time marks: you cannot start at a particular verse.
- A chapter is downloaded the first time you listen to it (3 to 12 MB, a few seconds depending on length and connection). The next chapter is fetched in the background already. Chapters you have heard stay in the cache and then play **without a network**. The cache holds at most 400 MB; the chapters not played for the longest time are deleted first.
- **Windows** needs nothing extra. On **macOS** the built-in `afplay` plays it; on **Linux** one of the players `mpv`, `ffplay`, `mpg123` or `cvlc` has to be installed. Jumping with the slider and the total duration exist on Windows only. Linux and macOS have not been tested much yet.

## 2.10 The bookmark star

Verses with a bookmark carry a star ★ next to the verse number. More on this in [Chapter 5](05-notes-highlights-bookmarks.md).
