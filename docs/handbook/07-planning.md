# 7. Planning: Reading List, To-dos, Prayers, Reading Plans, Memorizing, Languages

The **Planning** group in the right-hand rail holds everything that needs regularity. You can also reach the to-dos from anywhere with the **To-dos** button in the top bar.

## 7.1 Reading list

The reading list is a series of verses or passages that you want to work through **in order**.

- Add: right-click a verse → *To reading list*.
- The list shows the entries in your order; one click jumps to the passage.
- Finished items are checked off.

The reading list differs from a bookmark: the list has an **order and an end**, while bookmarks are loose markers.

## 7.2 To-dos

A to-do is a task for your study, optionally with a verse and a date. (Some of these used to be called "tasks"; now everything is called a **to-do**.)

### Create

- Type into the field at the top and press Enter, for example "Compare Romans 8".
- Or: right-click a verse → **Save as to-do**. The to-do is then called "Study passage" and attaches the verse.
- The same is available in the small window that the **To-dos** button at the top opens: type, press Enter, check off.

### Sub-to-dos, date, order

Using the **⋯** on a to-do, you can edit it, add a **sub-to-do**, set a **date**, or move it. You decide the order yourself. Delete removes a to-do together with its sub-to-dos.

### Progress and finished items

A progress indicator shows how much of a to-do with sub-to-dos is done. Finished to-dos collect in a section of their own; "Remove done" tidies it up.

## 7.3 Prayers

The prayer list keeps your **requests**, each with a date and, if you like, a verse.

1. Write the request at the top ("What would you like to pray or give thanks for?"). **Ctrl+Enter** adds it.
2. When a prayer is answered, you mark it as answered (**Prayer answered**) and can note **how** it was answered. Over time this builds into a look back.
3. A request can be deleted permanently (with a confirmation prompt).

Prayers can be found with **Ctrl+K**.

## 7.4 Reading plans

Reading plans spread the chapters evenly over the days. A chapter counts as read as soon as you mark it as **Read**, no matter where in the program you read. Plans are generated in the program; third-party content is not built in.

Available plans include:

- **Bible in a year** and more relaxed variants (all 1189 chapters, about 1 to 2 a day),
- **Pauline Epistles** (87 chapters from Romans to Philemon, about 3 a day),
- **Wisdom books** (Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs: 243 chapters),
- **Prophets** (Isaiah to Malachi: 261 chapters, about 3 a day),
- a **custom plan**: from book to book in as many days as you like (the first book must come before the last).

For each day, the plan shows the chapters; one button marks **all chapters of that day** as read. The program tracks your progress and your streak (consecutive days).

## 7.5 Memorize

1. Mark verses: right-click a verse → *For memorizing*, or add them in the *Memorize* tab.
2. Practice: the verse appears with **gaps**. The better you know it, the more words are missing. As a memory aid, the first letter and the punctuation stay in place ("God" becomes "G___").
3. The program uses **spaced repetition based on skill** (a box system like flashcards): what you knew comes back later, what you forgot comes back sooner.

The text comes from the translation you are reading in.

## 7.6 Learning languages: Greek and Hebrew

The **Languages** tab offers:

- the **alphabet** (Greek and Hebrew),
- a **Letter quiz**,
- a **vocabulary trainer** with spaced repetition based on skill. The words come from Strong's dictionary and are ordered by frequency and importance; the meanings are in English. When everything is learned for today, the program tells you so, and you can practice the alphabet.

## 7.7 Strategies: A weekly plan that lasts

- **10 minutes daily instead of 2 hours once.** A reading plan with 1 to 3 chapters a day is easier to keep up.
- **Read in the morning, review in the evening.** Memorization practice takes only a few minutes.
- **One request a day** in the prayer list; on Sundays, look through the answered prayers.
- **Keep to-dos small.** A to-do should be doable in one sitting ("Observe Rom 8:1–11" rather than "Understand the book of Romans").
- **Once a week**, go through the reading list and tidy up your bookmarks.

## 7.8 Statistics

The **Statistics** tab (group *Plan*) shows how much you have read:

- your **progress** through the whole Bible, the Old and the New Testament (chapters you marked as "Read" are counted),
- the **streak** (days in a row) and the longest streak, the chapters of the last 7 and 30 days and a bar chart of the last 12 weeks,
- how many notes, highlights, documents, bookmarks and prayers your vault holds, and how far you are with memorising,
- every book with progress as a bar.

The numbers are worked out on your computer from your vault only. The date on which a chapter was marked as read is stored; older marks without a date only count towards the progress.
