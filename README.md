## ✨ What you can do

| | |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Read and compare** | Many free translations, parallel columns, differences highlighted, an **audio Bible** that works offline |
| 🔎 **Search** | Words, phrases, base forms, Strong's numbers, people and places, words of Jesus only, topic search, a search builder, results on a map or as a graph |
| 🏛️ **Study the original** | Greek and Hebrew word by word, Strong's dictionary, grammar of every word, sentence structure, sentence diagrams |
| 🖍️ **Your own notes** | Highlights, notes, documents, folders, categories, tags, wiki links and a knowledge graph, in vaults like Obsidian |
| 👥 **Lead a Bible study** | Your Word/PDF/PowerPoint files inside the program with clickable Bible references, split view, handouts in eight looks, a projector view, a timer with a program for the evening |
| 🎤 **Sermons** | Editor with outline, verses, time plan, handout, slides and PDF export |
| 📅 **Plan** | Reading plans with progress, reading list, tasks, prayer list |
| 🎓 **Learn** | Greek and Hebrew alphabets, vocabulary trainer, verses by heart |
| 🗺️ **Explore** | Timeline with map, journeys, family tree |
| 🔄 **Sync and share** | Sync PC and phone, study together with another person (encrypted, no server), backups |
| 🧩 **Extend** | Plugins, five languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Russian), optional AI integration (MCP server for Claude) |

<details>
<summary><b>The full feature list</b></summary>

- **Read and compare:** many free translations, parallel columns (color-coded, optional synchronous scrolling), differences between translations highlighted, plus an **audio Bible** (spoken World English Bible, public domain, plays chapter after chapter and works offline once downloaded)
- **Search:** words, phrases, base forms, Strong's numbers, passages, search options (word beginning/ending, original-language words, proximity), **people and places** (`Person:Abraham`), **Old/New Testament references** (`Bezug:"Jer 31"`), **words of Jesus only**, a search builder, saved searches, many graph views (network, arcs, OT/NT, circle, writers, word/people/topic networks), journeys on the map, a Greek/Hebrew keyboard, clause search by syntactic roles, hits on the map, an OT/NT reference network and a **topic search**
- **Study:** word-by-word original text, Strong's dictionary, cross-references (graph, network, arc), Bible dictionary
- **Analysis tools:** morphology (every word's tense, case, person …) and **syntax** (clause structure with subject, verb, object …) for Greek and Hebrew; key words and unique words per chapter, related words, distribution of a word across the Bible
- **Sentence diagram and pericope analysis:** the sentence of a verse drawn as a classical sentence diagram (Reed–Kellogg, Greek and Hebrew, PNG/SVG export), and a passage in pictures: word classes, verb forms, key words and where they occur, rhythm of the verses, sentence build and links
- **Learn Greek and Hebrew:** alphabets, letter quiz, vocabulary trainer with spaced repetition
- **Timeline with map**, **family tree** of Bible people
- **Your own knowledge:** vaults (like Obsidian) with highlights (free text selection across verses, sub-highlights, one-click color change), notes, documents, folders, **categories**, tags, wiki links, study templates and a knowledge graph
- **Plan:** reading plans with progress, reading list, tasks, **prayer list**
- **Sermon editor** with outline, verses, time plan, handout, slides and print/PDF export
- **Bible study:** materials folder (Word, PDF, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, Markdown …) with clickable references, a verse preview when you rest the mouse on one, search across all files, marks and notes without changing the files; split view; handouts in eight looks; projector view; clock, timer and a program for the evening
- **Tabs like in a file explorer**, layouts (including a dashboard), custom keyboard shortcuts, animations
- **Multilingual:** English, German, Spanish, French and Russian (AI-made translations, corrections welcome; more languages can be added as a JSON file in the `lang` folder)
- **Study together:** connect directly with one other person (encrypted, no server, no account, free): chat, share verses and documents, follow each other's place; set up by swapping two short codes. It works best in the same network; across networks a private network such as ZeroTier or Tailscale, or your own relay server, can help (see the handbook)
- **Sync your devices:** PC, Android and iPhone keep each other up to date in the same Wi-Fi, or through a cloud folder (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox …)
- **Backups:** manual or scheduled, to a folder of your choice
- **AI integration (optional):** MCP server so that Claude can operate the program
- **Plugins:** extra translations, commands and program access (see [PLUGINS.md](PLUGINS.md)). Ready-made plugins are in the [`plugins`](plugins) folder and in each release, for example **Strong-Nummern ergänzen** (adds an *estimated* Strong's numbering to translations that have none; no guarantee, please check)

</details>

## 📥 Installation

> [!TIP]
> **Your data is safe during updates.** Installed on Windows it lives in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BibleStudy`, apart from the program. Updates and reinstalls never touch it, a backup is made before every update, and when you uninstall you are asked whether to keep it.

### Install on Windows

1. Download **`BibleStudy-Setup-x.y.z.exe`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/releases/latest).
2. Open it. No admin rights needed. The program updates itself from then on.

<sub>Prefer no installer? Take **`BibleStudy-Portable-x.y.z.zip`**, unpack it and run it. All data stays in the `Data` folder next to it.</sub>

### Install on macOS

1. Download **`BibleStudy-macOS-arm64-x.y.z.zip`** (Apple chip, M1 and newer) or **`BibleStudy-macOS-x64-x.y.z.zip`** (Intel) from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/releases/latest).
2. Unpack it and start **`Start-BibleStudy.command`**. The included `README-macOS.txt` explains the first start step by step.

### Install on Linux

1. Download **`BibleStudy-Linux-x64-x.y.z.tar.gz`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/releases/latest).
2. Unpack it and follow `README-Linux.txt`.

> [!NOTE]
> macOS and Linux packages are built on Windows and have been tried on a few machines only. If something does not start, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/issues).

### Install on Android

1. On the phone, download **`BibleStudy-Android-x.y.z.apk`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/releases/latest).
2. Open it and allow the install. The app updates itself from then on.

### Install on iPhone

**Free.** Apple lets only the App Store install apps, so BibleStudy comes through [SideStore](https://sidestore.io), which signs the app with your own free Apple ID. You need **a computer (Windows or Mac) and a cable once**. About 10 minutes in total.

**What you will do:** put SideStore on the iPhone with the computer → allow it on the iPhone → install BibleStudy → add one ready-made shortcut. That is all.

<table>
<tr><td>

**① Put SideStore on the iPhone** <sub>· on the computer · 4 min</sub>

1. Windows only: install **Apple Devices** (Microsoft Store) or **iTunes**. On a Mac nothing is needed.
2. Open **[iloader](https://github.com/nab138/iloader)** and connect the iPhone with the cable.
3. The iPhone asks **"Trust This Computer?"** → tap **Trust** and enter your passcode.
4. In iloader sign in with your Apple ID and install **SideStore**.

**Settings** → **General** → **VPN & Device Management** → under *Developer App* tap your Apple ID (your email address) → **Trust "…"** → **Trust**

</td></tr>
<tr><td>

**③ Turn on Developer Mode** <sub>· on the iPhone · 2 min</sub>

Without it, iOS will not open apps you signed yourself.

1. **Settings** → **Privacy & Security** → scroll all the way down → **Developer Mode** → switch on.
2. iOS asks to restart → tap **Restart**.
3. After the restart, unlock the iPhone. **"Turn On Developer Mode?"** appears → tap **Turn On** and enter your passcode.

> [!TIP]

</td></tr>

Get **LocalDevVPN** from the App Store, open it once and tap **Allow** for the VPN configuration.

<sub>SideStore talks to the iPhone through this tiny VPN on the phone itself. It sends no data anywhere. You never switch it yourself; step ⑥ does that.</sub>

</td></tr>
<tr><td>

**⑤ Install BibleStudy** <sub>· on the iPhone · 2 min</sub>

1. Open SideStore and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. On the iPhone tap **[this link](sidestore://source?url=https://trofline.github.io/BibleStudy/sidestore.json)**.<br><sub>Or in SideStore: **Sources** → **+** → `https://trofline.github.io/BibleStudy/sidestore.json`</sub>

<sub>You do **not** download the `.ipa` file yourself: through this source SideStore fetches it straight from the latest release, for every update too.</sub>

</td></tr>

2. Tap the download arrow at the top right → tap the file → it opens in **Shortcuts**.
3. Tap **Add Shortcut**. If iOS later asks whether it may use the VPN: **Always Allow**.

**Done.** From now on the VPN goes on when SideStore opens and off when it closes, and every 6 days the iPhone re-signs BibleStudy by itself, so the app never expires.

#### 🔄 Updating on the iPhone

BibleStudy **tells you** when a new version is out (a small notice in the app). It cannot install it by itself: iOS lets no app install another, so you do the update **by hand in SideStore**:

1. **Open SideStore** yourself (from the home screen; the button in the notice may open it for you).
2. Tap **Sources** at the bottom → **pull the list down** once, so SideStore looks for the new version.
3. Tap **My Apps** at the bottom → at **BibleStudy** tap **Update**.

<sub>Your data stays. If no **Update** shows yet, wait a few minutes and pull down in **Sources** again.</sub>

<details>
<summary><b>iPhone: problems and limits</b></summary>

- **The source cannot be added:** download `BibleStudy-iOS-x.y.z.ipa` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Trofline/BibleStudy/releases/latest) on the iPhone, then in SideStore under **My Apps** tap **+** at the top left and pick the file.
- **The app expired anyway:** open SideStore and tap **Refresh** at BibleStudy. Your data stays.
- **SideStore says the pairing file is missing:** open iloader again with the iPhone connected, click **Manage Pairing File** at the bottom and place it for SideStore.
- **Limits:** a free Apple ID allows at most 3 self-signed apps at once (SideStore counts as one). After a long time switched off or a big iOS update a manual refresh may be needed once. Apple may restrict this method one day. SideStore changes now and then: if a step looks different, [docs.sidestore.io](https://docs.sidestore.io) is the reference.
- **PC only:** Claude Code, Ollama and the MCP server. The live Bible study runs only while the app is open.

</details>

### Sync your devices

Once set up, PC, Android and iPhone keep each other up to date: marks, notes, documents, tasks, reading progress, plans, verses by heart and more. **No account, no server.**

1. On the PC: **Settings** → **Sync devices** → **Set up syncing**. A QR code appears.
2. Scan it with the phone's camera. BibleStudy opens and pairs.
3. A small green notice **"Connected to …"** confirms it, then **"Synced with …"** once everything is transferred. You never pair again.

In the same Wi-Fi the devices sync by themselves every few seconds. When **"Synced with …"** appears, you can close the app on the phone. Away from home, pick a **cloud folder** (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox …) in the same settings. Details: [handbook, chapter 19.3](docs/handbook/19-phone.md).
